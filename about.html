About Yoga1Hour - From Purification to Transformation

Yoga 1 Hour is built around the belief that quality practice begins with understanding the individual.

Why we started

To Move Beyond the Side-Effect Group Culture

Yoga 1 Hour was created to put quality, attention and the individual back at the centre of Yoga practice.

Instead of assuming that one general class fits everyone, our approach values personal observation, appropriate practice, consistency and a respectful learning environment.
