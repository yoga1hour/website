About Yoga 1 Hour

Yoga is an investment in human potential.

From individual health to institutional quality and a healthier nation.

Why Yoga 1 Hour started

To put the individual back at the centre of practice.

Yoga 1 Hour was created from a simple observation: people have different bodies, experiences, routines and goals, yet many classes are built around a general format.

Our approach is to create dedicated time for personal attention—at home for individuals and families, and in small groups for schools and workplaces. The aim is to help people build a practice they understand and can sustain.

We value the discipline of practice, the teacher-student relationship and the idea that Yoga should support a person's life rather than become another source of pressure.

Our approach

Personal attention, practical structure.

Individual needs

Practice can be discussed around age, mobility, experience, lifestyle, breathing patterns and personal goals.

Lineage & learning

We respect the teacher-student tradition and continue to learn through study, practice and responsible teaching.

Certificates & credentials

Verified certificates and professional credentials can be shared transparently during an enquiry. We do not publish unverified claims.

Books & study

We encourage study alongside practice and can share the relevant books, references and learning resources associated with the teaching approach.

A simple philosophy

Movement → Breath → Awareness → Calm → Focus.

The modern day can begin with messages, notifications and rush. We want the first hour to create a more intentional transition into work, learning and life.

About FAQ

Questions about our approach.

Why did you start Yoga 1 Hour?

To create a more individual, practical approach to Yoga and to make dedicated practice easier to bring into homes, schools and workplaces.

What makes your approach different?

We emphasise personal attention, a structured 60-minute session and adapting the practice to the participant or small group rather than relying only on a general sequence.

Can I see your certificates and lineage information?

Yes. Verified credentials and relevant learning information can be shared as part of an enquiry.
