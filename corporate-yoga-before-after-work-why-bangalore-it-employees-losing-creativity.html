Corporate Yoga Before & After Work in Bangalore - A Better Wellness Routine for IT Teams

Yoga 1 Hour · Bangalore

For HR managers and founders, the challenge is not simply offering yoga. It is making the wellness routine practical enough for employees to use.

Why Bangalore Corporate Life Creates a Wellness Challenge

Long screen hours, deadlines, meetings, commuting and constant digital stimulation can leave people physically sedentary and mentally overloaded. Employees can carry work stress into the evening, making recovery and family time harder.

Why an Office Session Can Work Better Than Another Commute

After a full workday, asking employees to travel to a crowded studio adds another transition. Bringing the teacher to the office removes that extra travel and lets the company schedule a session around its workday.

Before Work Yoga - 7:30 AM

A morning session can focus on movement, breathing, attention, mobility and a calm start before laptops and meetings begin.

After Work Yoga - 6:30 PM

An evening session can focus on releasing physical tension, breathing, meditation and a deliberate transition from office time to home time.

Small Groups for Better Attention

Yoga 1 Hour recommends small groups of approximately 5–8 employees per teacher. This keeps the program manageable for the company while allowing more individual observation than a very large class.

What HR Can Measure

Companies can evaluate participation, employee feedback, wellbeing experience and team engagement. The goal is a practical wellness routine that employees can sustain.
