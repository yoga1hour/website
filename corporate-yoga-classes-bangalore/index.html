Corporate Yoga Classes in Bangalore — De-Stress Before Work & After Work for Creative Teams

Small-group workplace Yoga sessions designed around the realities of Bangalore's workday.

The workplace challenge

Give employees a deliberate transition into and out of work.

Long screen hours, meetings, deadlines and commuting can leave little space for movement and recovery. Bringing Yoga to the workplace reduces another commute and creates a dedicated wellbeing window.

Sessions can focus on movement, comfortable breathing, awareness, relaxation and a calm transition—without promising medical outcomes.

Why small-group workplace Yoga?

More practical attention than a crowded general session.

After work

Small group

Corporate Yoga FAQ

Questions for HR teams.

What is Corporate Yoga in Bangalore?

It is a structured workplace Yoga program that brings movement, breathing and awareness sessions to an office or agreed workplace setting.

Can sessions be before or after work?

Yes. Scheduling is flexible and can be coordinated around the team's HR and office calendar.

How many employees can join?

Yoga 1 Hour recommends small groups for closer observation; a group size can be agreed according to the workplace and program design.
