Corporate Yoga FAQ

Questions for HR Teams

What is Corporate Yoga in Bangalore?

It is a structured workplace Yoga program that brings movement, breathing, awareness and relaxation sessions to an office or agreed workplace setting.

Can sessions be before or after work?

Yes. The program can be arranged around your team's HR and office calendar, with suggested morning and evening formats.

Why choose a small group of 5–8 employees?

A smaller group makes it easier for the teacher to observe participants and provide more individual attention within the workplace session.
