Private Yoga Classes at Home in Bangalore

Busy Bangalore life means traffic, long workdays and limited personal time. Home yoga removes the travel barrier and brings a personal yoga teacher to your doorstep at a convenient time.

Whether you are a beginner, working professional, parent or senior, a home session gives you a comfortable space to learn, practise and ask questions. The session can be adapted to your fitness, mobility, age, lifestyle, breathing pattern, experience and wellness goals.

Yoga 1 Hour focuses on one participant at a time, so the teacher can observe alignment, breathing and practice quality more closely than a large general class. The goal is a consistent one-hour routine that fits real life and helps you build a healthier relationship with your body and mind.

Specific Wellness Goals
