Home Yoga FAQ

Questions about private Home Yoga.

What is a private Home Yoga session?

It is a 60-minute, 1-to-1 Yoga session conducted in your home or another agreed personal space, with guidance adapted to your routine and goals.

Can beginners join Home Yoga?

Yes. Sessions can be planned around your experience and starting point, including beginners who want a structured routine.

How long is each session?

The standard session is 60 minutes. Scheduling is coordinated around your routine.
