Why Yoga is needed more than ever

One hour of Yoga can become the most valuable hour of the day.

The modern lifestyle brings constant digital stimulation, mental overload and distraction, long hours of work and study, increasing daily stress, sedentary lifestyles, reduced attention and mental freshness, and less time for meaningful self-care.

Against this background, a structured daily Yoga practice can provide a valuable opportunity to pause, breathe, move, center the mind and prepare the individual for the day.
