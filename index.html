07 — FAQ

Questions people ask before booking.

Is private Yoga better than studio Yoga?

It depends on the person and goal. Private Yoga offers more individual attention, privacy and scheduling flexibility, while group classes offer a shared environment. Yoga 1 Hour focuses on personal guidance at home.

What is the cost of a private Yoga teacher in Bangalore?

Yoga 1 Hour currently displays launch pricing on the Home Yoga page. Pricing is maintained centrally so future changes can be made without editing every page.

Do you come to Indiranagar, Whitefield, Jayanagar and other Bangalore areas?

We serve Bengaluru and can discuss availability in areas including Indiranagar, Whitefield, Jayanagar, Koramangala, HSR Layout, JP Nagar, Marathahalli, Sarjapur Road, Hebbal and Malleshwaram.

How quickly will I see progress compared with a group class?

There is no responsible one-size-fits-all timeline. Private guidance provides more opportunity for observation and adaptation, but progress depends on the individual, consistency and circumstances.
