Private Yoga at Home in Bangalore — Frequently Asked Questions

Is private yoga better than studio yoga?

Private yoga can provide more individual attention because the teacher can adapt the session to one participant's needs, experience and goals instead of teaching one general class format to a large group.

What is cost of private yoga teacher in Bangalore?

Pricing depends on the selected private plan, schedule and engagement. Contact Yoga 1 Hour for the current launch pricing and a plan suited to your needs.

Do you come to Indiranagar, Whitefield, Jayanagar etc?

Yes. We provide private yoga classes at home across Bangalore, including Indiranagar, Whitefield, Jayanagar, Koramangala, HSR Layout, JP Nagar, Marathahalli, Sarjapur Road, Hebbal and Malleshwaram, subject to teacher availability.

How fast will I heal compared to group?

There is no guaranteed healing timeline. Individual progress varies. The advantage of private practice is focused guidance, correction and a routine adapted to your needs.
