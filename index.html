Why Yoga is needed more than ever

One hour of Yoga can become the most valuable hour of the day.

The modern lifestyle brings constant digital stimulation, mental overload and distraction, long hours of work and study, increasing daily stress, sedentary lifestyles, reduced attention and mental freshness, and less time for meaningful self-care.

Against this background, a structured daily Yoga practice can provide a valuable opportunity to pause, breathe, move, center the mind and prepare the individual for the day.

Frequently asked questions

Yoga 1 Hour — quick answers.

What is Yoga 1 Hour?

Yoga 1 Hour is a structured 60-minute Yoga practice designed for individuals, schools and workplaces. Sessions focus on movement, breathing, awareness and preparation for the day.

Who can join Yoga 1 Hour?

Yoga 1 Hour offers formats for private individuals, schools and corporate teams. Personal sessions can be adapted to the participant’s needs, experience and routine.

Where is Yoga 1 Hour available?

The program currently serves Bengaluru and selected surrounding locations. Contact us to discuss your location and preferred session format.

How do I book a session?

Use the Book Now button or contact page. Your enquiry can be sent directly through WhatsApp for quick coordination.
