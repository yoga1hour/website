Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Yoga 1 Hour handles information when you visit yoga1hour.com, contact us, enquire about Yoga sessions, or otherwise interact with our website.

Last updated: 12 September 2026

It should be reviewed and updated if you add analytics, advertising, payment gateways, appointment software, embedded third-party services, newsletters, CRM systems, or other technologies that collect personal information.

1. Who we are

Yoga 1 Hour is a Yoga service website focused on structured Yoga practice and personal attention. The website describes services including home Yoga, school Yoga, corporate Yoga and different class types, and provides a way for visitors to make enquiries.

Website: https://yoga1hour.com/

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Your name and contact details when you make an enquiry.
  • Information about the Yoga service or class you are interested in.
  • Messages, questions, preferences or other details that you choose to provide.
  • Information you send to us through external communication services such as WhatsApp.

The website may also automatically receive limited technical information, such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from technical data, referring page and basic server or security logs. The exact information collected depends on the hosting provider and any services installed on the website.

We do not ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information through the website unless it is genuinely required for a specific service and appropriate notice is provided.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Discuss and arrange Yoga classes or related services.
  • Provide information about services requested by you.
  • Communicate with you about an enquiry or service.
  • Maintain, secure and improve the website.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, spam or security incidents.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

Yoga 1 Hour does not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. When information may be shared

We may share information only where reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide a requested service, for example with hosting, technical, communication or other service providers that process information on our behalf.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of Yoga 1 Hour, our users or others.

5. WhatsApp and external links

The website may provide a WhatsApp contact link for enquiries. When you choose to contact Yoga 1 Hour through WhatsApp, your interaction is handled by WhatsApp/Meta under its own terms and privacy practices. Please review the privacy information provided by the service before sharing personal information.

The website may also contain links to third-party websites or services. Yoga 1 Hour is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. Their own privacy policies apply.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your device. Yoga 1 Hour may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for website operation, security, preferences and, if enabled in the future, analytics or other functionality.

Essential technologies

Essential cookies or browser storage may be used when needed for basic website functionality, security, navigation or remembering a visitor's cookie-consent choice. These technologies do not necessarily identify you personally.

Analytics and advertising cookies

This website should not be treated as using analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookies unless those services have actually been installed. If Yoga 1 Hour later adds Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising tags, embedded marketing tools or similar technologies, this policy and the cookie-consent mechanism should be updated before those non-essential technologies are activated.

7. Cookie-consent storage

The cookie-consent banner supplied with this privacy page uses browser localStorage to remember the visitor's choice. This is browser storage, not a server-side database.

Storage key: yoga1hour_cookie_consent

Example stored value:

{
  "status": "accepted",
  "timestamp": "2026-09-12T06:30:00.000Z",
  "policyVersion": "1.0"
}

The possible consent statuses are accepted and rejected. The banner does not itself create advertising or analytics cookies.

If you later add a third-party service that sets its own cookies, configure that service so that non-essential cookies are loaded only after the visitor gives the appropriate consent.

8. Data retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide requested services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with legal obligations.

The exact retention period can vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is retained.

9. Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no website or method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction or deletion, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

You can also choose not to provide information through the website. Please note that some information may be necessary for Yoga 1 Hour to respond to an enquiry or provide a requested service.

11. Children's privacy

Yoga 1 Hour may provide Yoga services for school children, but the website is not intended to encourage children to independently submit personal information online. Parents, guardians, schools or authorised adults should make enquiries and provide information where appropriate.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, technology or applicable requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
