Private Yoga Classes at Home in Bangalore - Personal Yoga Teacher at Your Doorstep

One-to-one Yoga guidance at home, scheduled around your routine and shaped around your experience, needs and goals.

Private Home Yoga in Bangalore

A personal hour without the traffic.

Bangalore life can move quickly: long traffic-heavy commutes, demanding workdays, classes, family responsibilities and packed schedules can make it difficult to travel to a studio consistently. Private Yoga at home brings the practice to your doorstep, helping you use your time for movement, breathing, awareness and relaxation instead of another journey. In a one-to-one setting, the teacher can understand your routine, experience and goals and adapt the session accordingly. This is especially useful when you want personal guidance, closer observation and a practice that feels comfortable in your own space. Sessions can be arranged around your routine, whether before work, after work or at another mutually agreed time. The aim is not to rush through more postures, but to build a safe, understandable and consistent practice that you can continue with confidence over time.

Who is Private Home Yoga for?

A practice can start from where you are.

Different needs need different approaches

People may come with concerns such as back pain, thyroid conditions, PCOS, diabetes, anxiety or weight-loss goals. These are not interchangeable goals, so a Yoga session should not simply copy a one-size-fits-all group routine. Individual needs, comfort, experience and any medical guidance should be considered when planning practice.

Elderly, women & beginners

Home sessions can be a comfortable option for older adults, women and beginners who may feel shy, uncertain or more comfortable learning in a private setting. The teacher can explain movements and make appropriate adjustments throughout the hour.

Families with individual focus

A family can practise together at home while still receiving individual attention. Each person can work at their own level, with variations and corrections based on their experience and goals.

Why home Yoga can work faster for you

Focused guidance. Less guesswork. More consistency.

There is no universal promise of results in Yoga, but a private session can make the practice more focused and easier to personalise. The teacher has the full hour to observe your movement, listen to your feedback, correct technique and select breathing or relaxation practices that fit your current level.

  • 60 minutes dedicated to your individual practice.
  • Asana choices adapted to your experience, comfort and stated goals.
  • Personal correction and explanation instead of trying to keep pace with a group.
  • Customised breathing and awareness practices without forced breath holding.
  • Home scheduling can remove travel friction and support regular practice.

What happens in 1 hour?

A clear 60-minute structure.

10 MIN

Counselling & pulse/breath observation

Begin with a conversation about your routine, experience and goals, along with comfortable observation of breathing and movement. This is not a medical diagnosis.

30 MIN

Custom Asana practice

Practise postures and movements selected around your stated needs and level, with individual guidance and technique correction.

15 MIN

Pranayama & meditation

Practise comfortable breathing and meditation without forced breath retention, adjusted to your experience and comfort.

5 MIN

Ayurveda lifestyle tip

Close with one practical Ayurveda-inspired lifestyle suggestion for the day, suited to your routine and context.
