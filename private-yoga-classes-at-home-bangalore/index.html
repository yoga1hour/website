Private Home Yoga in Bangalore

A personal hour without the traffic.

Bangalore life can mean long commutes, busy workdays, family responsibilities and very little uninterrupted time for yourself. A home Yoga session removes the extra journey and gives you a dedicated hour in a familiar space. Yoga 1 Hour offers private sessions for people who prefer individual attention rather than following a general class. We begin by understanding your routine, experience and goals, then use an appropriate combination of movement, breathing, awareness and relaxation. Sessions can be scheduled in the morning before work, in the evening after work or at another mutually agreed time. The focus is not on doing the largest number of postures; it is on building a practice you can understand, perform comfortably and continue consistently.

Who is private Home Yoga for?

A practice can start from where you are.

Different goals

People may seek support for mobility, back comfort, stress management, sleep routines, fitness, weight-management goals or general wellbeing. Each person needs an appropriate approach.

Beginners & families

Home sessions can suit beginners, older adults and people who prefer a private environment. Families can coordinate sessions around their own routines.

Women & personal comfort

Discuss your preferred practitioner and setting when enquiring. Matching is subject to availability and scheduling.

Why personal attention?

More observation. More conversation. Less guesswork.

A private session gives the teacher more opportunity to observe technique, answer questions and adjust the session to the participant.

  • 60 minutes dedicated to one participant.
  • Movement and breathing selected around the person's context.
  • Technique and comfort can be discussed throughout the session.
  • Flexible home scheduling can make consistency easier.

What happens in 1 hour?

A clear 60-minute structure.

10 MIN

Conversation & observation

Discuss your routine, goals and current experience; observe comfortable breathing and movement.

30 MIN

Appropriate Asana

Practice movements and postures selected for your level and goals, with individual correction.

15 MIN

Pranayama & meditation

Comfortable breathing practice and a quiet awareness period without forcing breath retention.

5 MIN

Lifestyle note

Discuss one practical routine or Ayurveda-inspired lifestyle point appropriate to your context.
