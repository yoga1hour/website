Why early yoga matters

Why Yoga Must Be Mandatory in Early Age?

Educational Challenges

Children may struggle with focus, phone distraction, exam fear, anger or inconsistent routines. Yoga can create dedicated time for breathing, awareness and calm preparation.

Lifestyle Challenges

Screen time, junk food, heavy school bags, sedentary habits and irregular sleep can affect a child's daily wellbeing.

Small Batches

Small groups give a teacher more opportunity to observe each child rather than treating a large class as one uniform group.
