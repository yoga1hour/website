Why Yoga Must Be Mandatory for School Children in Bangalore - Focus, Health & Healthy Lifestyle

Yoga 1 Hour · Bangalore

Parents and schools increasingly look for practical ways to support attention, healthy routines and calm preparation during the school years.

Two Common Challenges for Bangalore School Children

Educational: distraction, phone overuse, exam fear, inconsistent attention and emotional overload. Lifestyle: screen time, junk food, sedentary habits, heavy school bags and irregular sleep.

Why a Large General Class May Not Fit Every Child

Children differ in age, confidence, mobility, attention and learning needs. A small batch gives the teacher more opportunity to observe each child and keep the practice age-appropriate.

What Our School Program Looks Like

Before school: a focused 45-minute routine combining movement, breathing and awareness. Batch size: maximum 10 children per teacher. Learning: balance, breathing, concentration, healthy habits and age-appropriate movement.

For Schools and Parents in Bangalore

Yoga 1 Hour offers school-focused programming and small-batch options for children at school or in suitable home/community settings. The program supports a healthy routine alongside education and family guidance.
