01 — QUICK ANSWER

What is Yoga 1 Hour school yoga?

It is a school-oriented yoga program designed around children rather than a general adult fitness class. Sessions can introduce simple asanas, age-appropriate pranayama, relaxation and awareness practices in a clear, supervised format.

The exact session structure can be discussed with the school based on age group, group size, available space, preferred timing and the school's objectives.

02 — WHY SCHOOL YOGA

A dedicated routine for movement, breath and awareness.

01

Age-Appropriate Practice

Activities can be selected and explained according to the children's age and experience instead of treating every participant as an adult practitioner.

02

Movement & Posture

Simple yoga movements can give children a structured opportunity to move, develop body awareness and practise comfortable positions.

03

Breathing & Calm

Age-appropriate breathing and relaxation practices can create dedicated time for children to slow down and notice their breath and surroundings.

04

Healthy Routines

School yoga can complement existing wellness activities by giving children a repeatable routine around movement, awareness and self-care.

03 — SESSION FORMAT

What can a school yoga session include?

01

Warm-Up & Movement

Simple movements help children become familiar with the session and prepare for the practice.

02

Yoga Asanas

Age-appropriate postures can be introduced with clear instructions and supervision.

03

Breathing & Relaxation

Suitable breathing, relaxation and awareness practices can be included according to the children's age.

04

Quiet Awareness

A short closing practice can help children transition back to their school routine in a calm and organised way.

The final sequence should always be agreed around the children's age, school environment and practical requirements.

04 — SCHOOL OBJECTIVES

Topics schools can build into the program.

  • Focus and attention during learning activities.
  • Healthy movement and body awareness.
  • Simple breathing and relaxation routines.
  • Screen-time balance and everyday self-care discussions.
  • Calm preparation around examination periods.
  • Positive, non-competitive participation in a group setting.

07 — FAQ

Questions schools and parents commonly ask.

What is school yoga?

School yoga is a structured program for children that can combine age-appropriate movement, breathing, relaxation, awareness and simple meditation practices in a supervised setting.

How many children can be included in a school yoga batch?

Yoga 1 Hour can discuss the school's preferred group size and format. Smaller batches can make it easier to supervise children and adapt activities to their age and level.

Can school yoga be scheduled before school or after school?

Yes. The timing can be discussed around the school's timetable, available space and the children's age group.

What does a school yoga session include?

Depending on the school and age group, sessions may include simple asanas, age-appropriate breathing, relaxation, awareness and meditation practices.

Which areas of Bangalore can you discuss for school yoga?
